Privacy Policy

Pacific Business Partners LLC respects your privacy. This policy describes how we handle information you share with us.

  • Information we receive: contact and business details you provide when you contact us, such as your name, business name, email address, and business information. If you apply through our Business Verification for Partnership page, we also receive the identification and business documents you submit there for verification purposes.
  • How we use it: solely to evaluate and discuss a potential business relationship and to respond to your inquiries. Verification documents are used only for legitimate business verification and partnership-related purposes.
  • Confidentiality: your business information is treated as confidential. We do not sell personal or business information to third parties. Access to submitted documents is restricted to authorized personnel.
  • What we never ask for: banking passwords, one-time passcodes, or account credentials. Never send these to us. Identity and business documents are only ever requested through our secure verification page — never by email, phone, or WhatsApp.
  • Data sharing: information is shared only where necessary to discuss an arrangement you have asked about, and never beyond that purpose without your knowledge.

For questions about this policy, contact us through the contact page.
