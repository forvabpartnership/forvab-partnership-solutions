                  Privacy Policy

                  Forvab Partnership Solutions respects your privacy. This policy describes how we handle information you share with us.

                  • Information we receive: contact and business details you provide when you contact us, such as your name, business name, email address, and business information.
                  • How we use it: solely to evaluate and discuss a potential business relationship and to respond to your inquiries.
                  • Confidentiality: your business information is treated as confidential. We do not sell personal or business information to third parties.
                  • What we never ask for: banking passwords, one-time passcodes, or account credentials. Never send these to us. Identity and business documents are only ever requested through this secure verification page — never by email, phone, or WhatsApp.
                  • Data sharing: information is shared only where necessary to discuss an arrangement you have asked about, and never beyond that purpose without your knowledge.
